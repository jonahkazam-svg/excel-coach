# Excel Coach — Study Vault

Your dedicated vault for learning Excel + financials (Breaking Into Wall Street).
**Separate from `AI-Tips-Vault` on purpose** — this is its own self-contained thing.

## What goes where
- **`Sessions/`** — your study-session transcripts (your voice; later, the instructor's audio too). Auto-created by the Whisper plugin when you stop a recording.
- **`Sessions/_screenshots/`** — screenshots captured during sessions (ShareX, ~60s timer + hotkey).
- **`Flashcards/`** — *(later)* spaced-repetition cards the coach generates.
- **`docs/`** — the design spec for this whole system.
- **`coach/`** — *(later)* the `/coach` tool that turns your sessions into quizzes, flashcards, and weakness reports.

## Where we are
**Phase 0 — capture setup.** Goal: hit record, narrate while you study, and get a clean transcript + screenshots in `Sessions/`. Build the habit first; the coach comes after ~1–2 weeks of real sessions.

The full plan and design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-06-09-voice-to-vault-study-coach-design.md`

## Privacy
Your sessions, screenshots, and Obsidian settings (including your OpenAI key) stay on your machine and are kept out of git.
